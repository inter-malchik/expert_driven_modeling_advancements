Gindullina et al.'s work (hereafter **Paper A**) treats “Compliance with the comment” as the primary success metric for Expert-Guided PINN: after an LLM rewrites the SIRD-PINN loss, authors visually decide whether the new incidence curve matches the expert’s text. Best models reach only 25–27% (6% for Llama-3.1-8B). Limitations frankly call this criterion subjective and Future work proposes an “LLM-expert-evaluator” without specifying what proposition that evaluator should check.

## Sources & Evidence

This section maps the card's claims to verbatim citations (English) from the local transcription: `2020 Contrastive Explanation A Structural-Model Approach.md`.

1) Claim: "Human explanations are typically contrastive rather than absolute; the real question is 'Why P rather than Q?'."
   - Evidence: Miller (2020), abstract / introduction.
   - Quotes (verbatim):
     > "explanations are contrastive: that is, when people ask for an explanation of an event — the fact — they (sometimes implicitly) are asking for an explanation relative to some contrast case; that is, 'Why P rather than Q?'."

     > "people do not ask 'Why P?'; they ask 'Why P rather than Q?', although often Q is implicit from the context."

2) Claim: "The contrast case reveals what the questioner expected and what they do not understand."
   - Evidence: Miller (2020), introduction.
   - Quotes (verbatim):
     > "people ask contrastive questions when they are surprised by an event and expected something different."

     > "The contrast case identifies what they expected to happen"

     > "This provides a ‘window’ into the questioner’s mental model, identifying what they do not know"

3) Claim: "Good contrastive explanations should focus on the differences between fact and foil, not all possible causes."
   - Evidence: Miller (2020), Difference Condition / discussion.
   - Quotes (verbatim):
     > "To explain why P rather than Q, we must cite a causal difference between P and not-Q"

     > "the explainer does not need to reason about or even know about all causes of the fact — only those relative to the contrast case."

     > "the explanation fits directly within the questioner’s ‘window’ of uncertainty, and is smaller and simpler"
