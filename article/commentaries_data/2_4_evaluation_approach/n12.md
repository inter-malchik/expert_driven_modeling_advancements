Huang et al.'s survey ties elevated sampling temperature directly to hallucination risk: more uniform token distributions increase tail-token sampling, exactly the mechanism behind the paper's own catalog of spurious variables and broken physics at T=1.0. Two of the survey's cited mitigations map onto data already produced at no extra cost: self-consistency scoring across the multiple T=1.0 samples per comment (currently discarded after one is shown to the expert), and LMvLM-style cross-examination; an examiner LLM questioning the generator; as a working instantiation of the paper's own proposed "LLM-expert-evaluator."
