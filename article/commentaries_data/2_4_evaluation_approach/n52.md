Epidemiological data often contains outliers or periods of high volatility, such as the start of a new variant wave. In Paper A, MAE thresholding and mean-residual optimization treat all time points with equal weight, so the model can ignore localized but critical failures if they affect only a small segment of the trajectory. That is especially dangerous for expert requests about 7-10 day peak shifts, where a local residual error can still produce a globally plausible but semantically wrong forecast.

## Sources & Evidence

This section maps the card's claims to verbatim citations (English) from local full-text transcriptions in `article/transcriptions/`.

1) Claim: "Mean residual objectives can hide large localized errors; RRaPINNs reframes PINN training as tail-focused, risk-sensitive optimization using CVaR."
   - Evidence: Akazan et al. (2025), `2025 RRaPINNs: Residual Risk-Aware Physics Informed Neural Networks.md`, §1 Introduction; §4.1.2.
   - Quotes (verbatim):
     > "The objective (7) controls only the mean residual and can hide large localized errors, which may deteriorate the approximation performance." (§4.1, lines 107-109)

     > "We bring this risk-averse perspective directly to the physics loss in PINNs, thereby introducing Residual Risk-Aware PINNs (RRaPINNs)." (§1, lines 31-31)

     > "By directly penalizing the average severity of these extreme events, CVaR provides a principled, optimization-friendly surrogate for robust residual control." (§4.1.2, lines 152-153)

2) Claim: "RRaPINNs explicitly targets worst-case / tail residual behavior and exposes a transparent trade-off knob through the reliability level alpha."
   - Evidence: Akazan et al. (2025), `2025 RRaPINNs: Residual Risk-Aware Physics Informed Neural Networks.md`, §1 Introduction; §4.3; §6 Conclusion.
   - Quotes (verbatim):
     > "The final loss (28) exhibits the weighting system explicitly, which is now a residual risk aware mean that shifts training focus onto the most problematic regions of the PDE domain in each batch." (§4.3.1, lines 274-275)

     > "they can be viewed as a drop-in modification of the residual loss with a small, interpretable set of knobs (notably the reliability level α) that trades bulk accuracy (lower α) against tail control (higher α)." (§6, lines 633-634)

     > "In our experiments, the mean-excess (ME) surrogate of the CVaR was numerically more stable and achieved stronger tail control than a direct CVaR hinge." (§6, lines 634-634)

3) Claim: "Residual-Quantile Adjustment focuses adaptive weighting on the tail of the residual distribution by clipping weights above a quantile threshold to the median."
   - Evidence: Han et al. (2022), `2022 Residual-Quantile Adjustment for Adaptive Training of Physics-informed Neural Network.md`, Abstract; §I Introduction; §IV.B Quantile adjustment.
   - Quotes (verbatim):
     > "we show that the bottleneck in the adaptive choice of samples for training efficiency is the behavior of the tail distribution of the numerical residual." (Abstract, lines 31-33)

     > "Since the residuals take a very high value near these singular locations, the distribution of the residuals typically has a long tail, which prevents the efficiency decrease in the mean square error." (§I Introduction, lines 51-52)

     > "for the weights larger than the quantile, their weights are considered to be too large, so we adjust the weights to a proper number (for example, the median of all weights)." (§IV.B, lines 262-264)

     > "We name this method the “Residual-Quantile adjustment” (RQA)." (§IV.B, line 264)
