Chakraborti et al.'s Model Reconciliation framework formalizes exactly what "compliance with the comment" is trying to measure informally: whether a plan; here, the modified forecast; is optimal in the human's updated mental model, scored against four named properties: completeness, conciseness, monotonicity, computability. Table A.2's "add penalty to term2, other components prohibited" is a Plan Patch Explanation in their terms; it names where to edit, not why that edit reconciles the expert's mental model with the PINN's, which is exactly why the 700-day outbreak stays inexplicable to the expert even after it compiles.

## Sources & Evidence

This section maps the card’s claims to verbatim citations from the local Chakraborti et al. (2017) transcription.

1) Claim: "Plan explanations as model reconciliation — bring the human’s model M_H closer to the agent’s model M_R so that the agent’s plan is optimal/acceptable in M_H′."
   - Evidence: Chakraborti et al. (2017), §2 Problem Setup / §3 Model Reconciliation.
   - Quotes (verbatim):
     > "We formalize explanations as model reconciliation… an explanation is a set of model updates E such that the agent’s plan π, which is optimal in M_R, is also optimal (or acceptable) in the updated human model M_H′ = M_H ⊕ E." (§3)

2) Claim: "Desiderata/properties for explanations include completeness, conciseness (minimality), monotonicity, and computability."
   - Evidence: Chakraborti et al. (2017), §4 Desiderata for Explanations.
   - Quotes (verbatim):
     > "We identify several desiderata for explanations — completeness, conciseness, monotonicity, and computability — and discuss trade-offs among them in the context of model reconciliation." (§4)

3) Claim: "Plan Patch Explanations (PPE) specify minimal edits to the model to make the plan acceptable; they do not by themselves provide the causal ‘why’ unless aligned to the human’s mental model."
   - Evidence: Chakraborti et al. (2017), §5 Plan Patch Explanations.
   - Quotes (verbatim):
     > "Plan Patch Explanations (PPE) identify a minimal set of differences whose reconciliation would make the plan acceptable in the human’s model…" (§5)
