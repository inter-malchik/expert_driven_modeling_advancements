Gindullina et al. (hereafter **Paper A**) treat “Compliance with the comment” as a binary, author-judged primary success metric (25%/27%/6% for Llama-70B / DeepSeek / Llama-8B) and plan a future LLM-expert-evaluator without modeling *who* accepts an edit or *how deeply* they check it. Limitations already flag subjectivity; Future work still averages success as if all expert judgments were interchangeable.

## Sources & Evidence

This section maps the card’s claims to verbatim citations (English) from local transcriptions.

1) Claim: "Argument acceptance is modeled via flags (accepted ⊕, rejected −, unspecified .) within a cognitive KB; acceptance is an agent-level event."
   - Evidence: Bisquert, Croitoru, Dupin de Saint-Cyr (2015), Definition 2 (Knowledge Base); lines 66–71 in `article/transcriptions/2015 Towards a Dual Process Cognitive Model for Argument Evaluation.md`
   - Quotes (verbatim):
     > "A knowledge base KB ... is a quadruplet KB=(F,Δ,A,R) ... An appreciation is a pair (ϕ,f)∈L×{⊕,−,.} meaning that ϕ is associated to the flag f."

2) Claim: "Reasoning is the process of evaluating acceptability of a formula by mapping it to a flag; S1 vs S2 differ in depth of checking."
   - Evidence: Bisquert et al. (2015), §2.2 System 1 and System 2 Reasoning; lines 105–106
   - Quotes (verbatim):
     > "we call reasoning the process of evaluating the acceptability of a formula ϕ∈L, i.e., mapping ϕ to a flag in {⊕,−,.}. The reasoning is not the same in S1 and S2."

3) Claim: "Dual-process model: S1 is heuristic/superficial/fast; S2 is scrupulous/thorough/slow — implying heterogeneous ‘depth’ of acceptance checks."
   - Evidence: Bisquert et al. (2015), §1 Introduction; line 34
   - Quotes (verbatim):
     > "reasoning may be achieved thanks to two different processes, one being heuristic, superficial and fast, and the other being scrupulous, thorough and slow."
